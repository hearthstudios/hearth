Our story

We build the conditions where talent thrives.

HEARTH is a for-profit social enterprise and community-powered studio in NYC and NJ, working nationally across rotating cities.

Our mission

Why we exist

HEARTH makes the craft of storytelling accessible to creators building outside traditional gates, particularly Black, POC, LGBTQ+, disabled, and working-class artists. We provide classes, workshops, residencies, mentorship, and cultural events.
