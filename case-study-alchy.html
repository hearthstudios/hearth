Authentic, in the moment.The challenge

Capture customers interacting with the brand in real time. Not staged, not scripted. Authentic.

02

Plan first. Then show up fully.Our approach

A clear plan with intentional concepts mapped before a single frame was shot. We handled release waivers, engaged the public directly, and stayed on site from start to finish.

Alchy Cocktails received five videos that exceeded expectations, with concepts captured in great detail. All five went straight to social media, earning approximately 73K views and 613 likes.
