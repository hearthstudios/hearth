Spoken & Sung · Artist call · Rolling

Fifteen minutes. Your work. On camera.

Spoken & Sung is HEARTH’s filmed performance series in New York. We’re booking singers, rappers, bands, poets and spoken word artists to perform a 15-minute set of original work. You leave with the full footage.

FAQ

Still have a question? Email operations@buildyourhearth.studio.

Is it paid?

Not right now. Performances are unpaid, and you keep the full footage of your set. If future nights are ticketed, we’ll share performer terms before anyone is booked.

Who owns the footage?

HEARTH keeps the rights to the Spoken & Sung series. You get the full footage of your set to post, pitch with, and use however you like.

Can I perform covers?

No. Original work only, and you need to own the rights to everything you perform. That keeps the series clear to release.

How long is a set?

15 minutes.

Will there be an audience?

It depends on the night. Some sessions may be filmed in front of a live room, others in a closed studio. We’ll tell you which when you’re booked.

Do I need a following?

No. If you can bring people out, great, but it’s never a requirement.

How do I hear back?

We review applications on a rolling basis and reach out by email if it’s a fit.

Can I also submit to the sync library?

Sync library submissions are paused while we review the current queue. Get notified when they reopen.
